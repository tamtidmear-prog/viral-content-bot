# Ai_In_Mind — Image Prompt Template

> ใช้ template นี้ทุกครั้งที่สร้างรูปผ่าน ChatGPT Images pipeline

---

## Structure

```
[FACE REF LINE]
[SCENE DESCRIPTION]
[OUTFIT + ACCESSORIES]
[BACKGROUND + MOOD]
[QUALITY LINE]  ← ต้องมีทุกโพส
```

---

## QUALITY LINE (บังคับ — ใส่ท้ายทุก prompt)

```
Photo quality: 4K ultra-high resolution, shot on professional DSLR camera (Canon EOS R5 or Sony A7R V Mark II), commercial fashion model photography, tack-sharp focus throughout, true-to-life skin texture and hair detail, professional studio-grade natural lighting, editorial quality comparable to Vogue or Harper's Bazaar fashion shoots. Photorealistic, not AI-generated looking. No text, no logos, no brand names.
```

---

## FACE REF LINE (บังคับ — ใส่บนสุดทุก prompt)

```
Use the uploaded face as face reference. Keep the exact same oval face shape, almond eyes, and facial features. Do not alter the face structure.
```

---

## ตัวอย่าง prompt เต็ม

```
Use the uploaded face as face reference. Keep the exact same oval face shape, almond eyes, and facial features. Do not alter the face structure.

A young Thai woman in her mid-20s [SCENE]. She wears [OUTFIT]. [HAIR]. [ACCESSORIES].

Background: [SETTING]. Mood: [MOOD].

Photo quality: 4K ultra-high resolution, shot on professional DSLR camera (Canon EOS R5 or Sony A7R V Mark II), commercial fashion model photography, tack-sharp focus throughout, true-to-life skin texture and hair detail, professional studio-grade natural lighting, editorial quality comparable to Vogue or Harper's Bazaar fashion shoots. Photorealistic, not AI-generated looking. No text, no logos, no brand names.
```
