## Verdict Summary

1. **OpenAI Dreaming memory update** — **PARTIAL / mostly TRUE**
   - TRUE: OpenAI published "Dreaming: Better memory for a more helpful ChatGPT" on June 4, 2026.
   - TRUE: It is a more capable memory synthesis system for freshness, continuity, relevance, and multi-year time horizons.
   - TRUE: OpenAI says compute required to serve dreaming to Free users dropped about 5x, enabling Free rollout over coming weeks and increased capacity for Plus/Pro.
   - TRUE but vendor/internal eval: factual recall 41.5% -> 82.8% appears in OpenAI result snippets/charts, but methodology is not independently verifiable.
   - CAUTION: Official page says "dreaming"; "Dreaming V3" is common secondary shorthand, not the main official headline wording in the fetched text.
   - CAUTION: Do not say Free users get "memory เต็มรูปแบบทันที"; safer: "เริ่มทยอยเปิดให้ Free users ในช่วงไม่กี่สัปดาห์ข้างหน้า."
   - Source: https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/

2. **ลบ ChatGPT memory ต้องลบ 2 ที่** — **TRUE**
   - OpenAI Help says deleting a chat does not remove saved memory.
   - To fully remove a memory, delete both the saved memory in Manage memories and the chat where it was originally shared.
   - Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-faq

3. **Anthropic "When AI builds itself"** — **PARTIAL / mostly TRUE**
   - TRUE: Anthropic says as of May 2026, more than 80% of code merged into Anthropic's codebase was authored by Claude.
   - TRUE: In Q2 2026, typical engineer merged 8x as much code per day as in 2024.
   - TRUE: Anthropic argues a credible slowdown/pause would require frontier labs in multiple countries and verifiable coordination.
   - CAUTION: I did not find "US + China" as explicit wording in the Anthropic article. Safer wording: "หลายประเทศ/หลาย frontier labs พร้อมกัน และตรวจสอบได้."
   - Source: https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement

4. **SpaceX IPO: Nasdaq SPCX, $135, $75B, ~$1.75T, loss, xAI merger** — **TRUE**
   - SEC filing supports 555,555,555 Class A shares at anticipated $135/share = ~$75B gross.
   - SEC filing says shares expected to trade on Nasdaq under ticker SPCX; FAQ says final price June 11 and trading June 12.
   - SEC filing says SpaceX acquired xAI effective February 2, 2026.
   - SEC filing says 2025 net loss was $4.937B and Q1 2026 net loss was $4.276B.
   - Valuation ~$1.75T/$1.77T is mathematically consistent with offering/share count and reported coverage; cite carefully as "ประมาณ".
   - Source: SEC filing https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026040610/spacexfwp.htm
   - NOTE: This is finance content. Keep the caution paragraph and avoid sounding like buy/sell advice.

5. **Great American AI Act** — **TRUE**
   - Official Obernolte release says Obernolte and Trahan released a discussion draft on June 4, 2026; not formally introduced yet.
   - DLA Piper analysis says draft spans 269 pages.
   - It requires frontier model frameworks/transparency and independent verification organizations/audits.
   - Audits are semi-annual and ad hoc at CAISI request.
   - Title I preempts state laws specifically regulating AI model development for 3 years unless reauthorized; does not apply post-deployment or general applicability laws.
   - Workforce transparency around AI as a substantial factor in qualifying mass layoffs is supported.
   - Sources:
     - https://obernolte.house.gov/media/press-releases/obernolte-trahan-release-discussion-draft-great-american-ai-act
     - https://www.dlapiper.com/en-au/insights/publications/2026/06/unpacking-the-great-american-ai-act
